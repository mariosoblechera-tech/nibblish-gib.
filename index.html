About us

BORN FROM PASSION

Nibblish by Itané brings the best of Roman street food culture to the heart of Gibraltar. We believe food should be bold, authentic, and made with love — using only the finest Italian ingredients fused with the vibrant flavours of our community.

From our 72-hour fermented pinsa dough to our handcrafted supplì and arancine, every dish tells a story. Come hungry. Leave happy.
